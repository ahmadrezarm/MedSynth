The Problem We're Solving

⏰
Documentation Burden

Electronic health records (EHRs) introduce a significant documentation burden. Physicians report spending 52–102 minutes daily on clinical note-taking from patient encounters, detracting from direct patient care.

😓
Physician Burnout

This extensive documentation requirement contributes significantly to physician burnout, highlighting an urgent need for solutions that can mitigate these demands while preserving quality of patient records.

🔒
Data Scarcity

Developing automated documentation tools is hampered by the lack of large, open-access datasets that are both comprehensive and privacy-compliant. Existing datasets are limited in scope, lack standard medical formats, or are unavailable.

📋
Format Standardization

Medical notes should adhere to standards like SOAP format (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) to ensure continuity of care. Many existing datasets lack this structure, limiting their utility.

MedSynth provides a valuable resource for training and evaluating medical dialogue and note generation models, addressing the critical need for privacy-compliant healthcare NLP datasets.

Abstract

Physicians spend significant time documenting clinical encounters, a burden that contributes to professional burnout. To address this, robust automation tools for medical documentation are crucial. We introduce MedSynth — a novel dataset of synthetic medical dialogues and notes designed to advance the Dialogue-to-Note (Dial-2-Note) and Note-to-Dialogue (Note-2-Dial) tasks.

Informed by an extensive analysis of disease distributions, this dataset includes over 10,000 dialogue-note pairs covering over 2,000 ICD-10 codes. We demonstrate that our dataset markedly enhances the performance of models in generating medical notes from dialogues, and dialogues from medical notes. The dataset provides a valuable resource in a field where open-access, privacy-compliant, and diverse training data are scarce.

Key Contributions

📊 Large-Scale Dataset
Over 10,000 synthetic dialogue-note pairs covering 2,000+ ICD-10 codes with SOAP-formatted notes.
🔄 Novel Pipeline
Multi-agent LLM pipeline for generating realistic, validated medical dialogue-note pairs with quality control.
🏆 State-of-the-Art
Fine-tuned models achieving SotA performance in Dial-2-Note and Note-2-Dial tasks.

Data Generation Pipeline

Our pipeline uses multiple LLM agents with Chain-of-Thought prompting and In-Context Learning. We first generate SOAP-formatted notes, then use these notes to generate corresponding dialogues.

📝 Note Generation Pipeline

4-agent system with Chain-of-Thought prompting

1
Scenario Provider
Receives ICD-10 description, selects physician role, generates scenario with 13 clinical variables
↓
2
Scenario Judge
Validates medical accuracy, checks plausibility, ensures diversity from previous scenarios
↓
3
Note Writer
Generates SOAP-formatted clinical note using One-Shot ICL with Aci-Bench examples
↓
4
Note Polisher
Refines note, ensures correct section placement, adds missing referral details

💬 Dialogue Generation Pipeline

2-agent system with Few-Shot In-Context Learning

1
Dialogue Generator
Generates doctor-patient dialogue from medical note using Few-Shot ICL (n=3 examples)
↓
2
Dialogue Polisher
Adds realistic social chatter, verifies all note information is included, reduces hallucinations
Quality Variables: Our notes include 13 carefully selected variables based on expert survey: Medical Outcome, Medical History, Symptom Description, Patient Lifestyle, Demographics, Patient Behavior, Geography, Clinical Setting, Encounter Type, Treatment Disparities, Language, Physical Exams, and Investigation Results.

Experimental Results

🧪 LLM Jury Evaluation

Traditional metrics like BLEU and ROUGE don't correlate well with human judgment for medical text evaluation. We use an LLM jury with majority voting from three diverse models: Prometheus, GPT-4o, and Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct.

Dial-2-Note Rubric: Hallucination, Critical Omissions, Professional Tone, Logical Structure, SOAP Adherence, Section Relevance
Note-2-Dial Rubric: Completeness, Accuracy, Naturalness & Flow, Medical Terminology, Evidence-Based Support

📝 Dial-2-Note Task

LLM-based evaluation on Aci-Bench test set. Jury Preference Rate shows how often the jury of LLM judges preferred MedSynth-trained models.

Comparison Training Samples Jury Pref. Rate
MedSynth+Aci vs. NoteChat+Aci 10,102 60.0%

💬 Note-2-Dial Task

Generating realistic dialogues from clinical notes. Results show consistent improvement when MedSynth is included in training.

Comparison Training Samples Jury Pref. Rate
MedSynth+Aci vs. NoteChat+Aci 10,102 55.0%
MedSynth Only vs. NoteChat Only 10,035 87.5%
MedSynth+Aci vs. Aci Only 10,102 80.0%
MedSynth+Aci vs. PriMock+Aci 124 57.5%
MedSynth Only vs. PriMock Only 57 97.5%
💡
Key Finding

When trained exclusively on MedSynth vs. NoteChat, the performance gap widens significantly (95% vs 87.5% preference). Models trained only on NoteChat failed to maintain SOAP format, a critical component of standard medical documentation.
